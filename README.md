# HR Analytics – Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition patterns using Microsoft Excel. The objective is to understand employee turnover, identify factors associated with attrition, and generate meaningful HR insights to support data-driven decision-making.

## Dataset Information

- Total Employees: 1,470
- Employees Who Left: 237
- Employees Who Stayed: 1,233
- Overall Attrition Rate: 16.12

## Key Findings

- Overall employee attrition rate is 16.12%.
- Single employees have the highest attrition rate at 25.53%.
- Employees with zero training sessions have an attrition rate of 28%.
- Employees with Stock Option Level 0 have the highest attrition rate at 24.41%.
- Employee tenure and job satisfaction show almost no linear correlation.
- Years since last promotion and performance rating also show almost no linear correlation.

## Recommendations

- Improve employee engagement and retention strategies.
- Review employee training and development programs.
- Evaluate employee benefits and reward structures.
- Conduct regular employee feedback sessions.
- Monitor attrition trends across departments and job roles.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to analyze employee attrition and identify important HR trends. The findings can help HR teams understand employee turnover patterns and develop better employee retention strategies.

**Note:** The findings indicate associations and patterns in the dataset. They do not establish direct causes of employee attrition.
